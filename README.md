# Shadowrun map converter

## What is it ?
The goal of this project is to provide a tool that convert all of a city's Points of Interest (POIs) into Shadowrun POIs. For exemple, big corporate buildings become Big Twelve HQs, hospitals and medical amenities become DocWagon and streetdoc workplaces...

## Why ?
I really like GMing Shadowrun Anarchy and thought it would be fun to run campaings in environments that are more familiar to my players than Seattle or other big city we can find in the lore books of the 6th world. So I got the idea of mapping Shadowrun to the real world. It is not perfect, because the events in the lore changed a lot of the geography, but I think the result can be quite OK for a neighborhood or a small city.

## How does it works ?
Well, it doesn't really work right now. I'm in the process of building it.
Currently, it can fetch any city on the globe and extract it's POIs... and that's it. The next step will be to convert them and generate a map of the city that can be displayed and interacted with.
